04 — Yield & Upsell

Sell the same evening
at four different prices.

Tiered inventory is where coastal margin actually lives. The same sunset event is sold as a 450 SAR shoreline pass, an 1,100 SAR VIP high-deck seat, and a 7,500 SAR private majlis for eight — with optional concierge and catering attached at checkout.

  • Scarcity is real, not theatrical. "Only 2 left" is drawn from the same capacity counter that the door check-in decrements.
  • Add-ons at the point of intent. Priority valet, caviar platters, and jet-ski packages attach to the order before payment, not after.
  • VAT computed inline. The guest sees the 15% ZATCA line and the true total before committing — no surprise at the payment step.

  • ZATCA-ready VAT invoicing. 15% computed and displayed on every order line, carried through to the printable order record.

  • Explicit privacy consent. Terms and privacy acknowledgement captured before payment is authorised.
